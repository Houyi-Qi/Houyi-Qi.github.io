I study trustworthy collaboration and secure interaction among heterogeneous intelligent agents in AI-native networks, with a focus on trustworthy interaction, security and privacy, and risk governance. My work develops game-theoretic, matching-theoretic, and learning-enabled mechanisms for reliable coordination and service interaction, aiming to advance intelligent networks toward secure, trustworthy, and controllable operation.
